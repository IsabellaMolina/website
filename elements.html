Competency Two

Creating Effective and Inclusive Learning Environments

Description of Competency

Teaching via a traditional approach with class time being designated for lectures and tests does not effectively teach all students. To serve a diverse community of students, teachers need to use a myriad of instructional methods. Using in class I-clicker questions encourages class participation while removing the fear of answering incorrectly. Group work and think-pair-share allows students to learn from each other and makes the learning experience communal. Using projects and real world problems makes the course work relevant to students and allows them to show their mastery of the material and concepts in different ways.

Artifacts and Rationale

To learn more about creating effective learning environments I attended the Certificate in College Teaching Institute in 2025 and participated in a workshop on the topic. From this workshop I learned about equitable grading, how to use pre class assignments to prepare students for class and to give students clear examples of a "final product" so that they know what they are working towards.

Students in higher level courses can be asked to show mastery of the material by designing and creating original work. Students in an intro course can show an understanding of the material by applying their knowledge to solve problems. Creating effective learning environments requires a lot of planning and work prior to class. By using learning goals you can effectively plan your teaching to ensure you cover the required topics and that students are able to work with the material at the appropriate level.

    Reflections

    Creating an effective and inclusive learning environment is critical for the learning and wellbeing of my students. I cannot effectively teach my students if they do not feel safe or welcome in my class. First generation students may not be aware of expectations or norms in a college classroom and so I will need to be clear in what students should expect from me and what I will expect of them. Details like how long you should be spending on homework, where you can find resources for support, and explaining what office hours are will help all students understand when they should seek help and how to find it. Students should clearly know what it means to be successful in my class and understand their grade at the end of it.

    I will also be transparent about my own academic journey so that students understand that mistakes are part of the process, not a sign that they aren’t cut out for the course. As an educator I must be introspective about my teaching practice and honest about my effectiveness. Students need to feel comfortable enough to give me this valuable feedback. This requires trust and connection, so I must be honest about my myself, my struggles and my learning experience. I can model to my students what they can accomplish with perseverance and hard work and show them that mistakes are part of the process. In order to test my efficacy I will also use student surveys to get feedback on my teaching methods. This will help me grow as an educator and craft lesson plans that are most effective.

    Cooperative learning will be used in my classroom so students will be working together in groups. However, this approach requires structure and each student will have a role to play in the group. There will be guidelines for the groups as well as regular self assessment of the health of the group. On the first day we will introduce ourselves, discuss expectations of group work and each group will devise their own set of norms and rules that they will all abide by. In addition to this, there will be a way to deal with conflict should it arise. The goal for my classroom is that all students will be engaged and learn how to work together to solve problems.

    Problems and lessons will be context rich, drawing from different backgrounds and experiences that students may have. My intention is for students to begin to see how this work is relevant to their own lives and how they can use what they learn in my class outside of the classroom. I will also use pre class assignments to familiarize students with concepts prior to class. This material will be short, a 2-4 minute video, or 5 minute reading assignment with some reflection questions. The goal is to get students thinking of the material before they get to class so we can start working on problems together.
