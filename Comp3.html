Competency Three

Incorporating Technology in Teaching

Description of Competency

Incorporating different technology in my classroom can aid in making my teaching more effective and my classroom more inclusive. Whether being used in a hybrid or online course or a traditional in person lecture, using the tools available to better present and assess knowledge is critical for creating an optimal teaching environment.

One tool I plan to use is pre class assignments. These will be administered online prior to class as a way of giving students a preview of what we will cover during the lesson. They will consists of a short reading online or a video to watch prior to class. Students then are required to post one question they had about the reading or video. The purpose of this assignment is to get students thinking about the material prior to class. In the class room I use I-clicker questions during the lecture to keep students engaged and to check for understanding. If a large portion of the class does not respond correctly this lets me know that I need to spend more time on that topic. Giving students anonymity allows them to answer questions without fear of being incorrect allowing more students to participate. During group work students will work on problems together and use whiteboards to share ideas and organize information. This makes problem solving more communal and easily allows students to adjust their approach or correct mistakes. Using backwards design makes it easier to create a lesson using technology. In order to have engaging activities I need a more structured format for my lesson planning so that I can come to class with a cohesive message and goal.

    Reflection

    Teaching physics and astronomy allows me to use physical demonstrations of concepts easily. This approach to learning can increase my reach of students who learn differently from traditional classroom approaches. Technology I can use includes tools like the PHet interactive simulations from University of Colorado Boulder (Link Here). In an astronomy gravity and orbits lab I used Phet to help students visualize and play with planetary orbits.This allowed students to test models of understanding and see what happens in the extreme cases, furthering their knowledge.

    Using backwards design is key to make sure I am using tech in an effective impactful way. Using technology just for the sake of using technology does not mean that it will aid student learning and may be an additional barrier to students who do not have access to tech. While TA'ing for an intro astronomy lab, one of the labs required students complete and submit the lab online. However, this design ignored the fact that the classroom did not have reliable wi-fi and resulted in many frustrated students and me and my fellow TA's playing the role of tech support to students. Certain kinds of technology require their own considerations and when planning a new lesson using tech it is beneficial to run the lesson in the classroom to work out any issues before the lesson is taught. Technology is also useful for having check-ins with students, assessing their understanding in formative and summative assesments (like using i-clicker questions) and for getting feedback from students on my teaching.
