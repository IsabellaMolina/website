    Developing Discipline Related Teaching Strategies

    The focus of this competency was to learn teaching strategies specific to your discipline. To learn how to develope these teaching strategies I took the Center for the Integration of Research, Teaching and Learning (CIRTL) Massive Open Online Courses (MOOCs) course “Introduction to Evidence Based Undergraduate STEM Teaching”. This course is meant to be an introduction to best teaching practices in STEM. Teaching Physics and Astronomy to students will come with its own unique challenges. Students in an astronomy or physics course may not believe the material is relevant to them or feel overwhelmed in a science class that requires more math.

    Creating Effective and Inclusive Teaching Environments

    Teaching via a traditional approach with class time being designated for lectures and tests does not effectively teach all students. To serve a diverse community of students, teachers need to use a myriad of instructional methods. Using in class I-clicker questions encourages class participation while removing the fear of answering incorrectly. Group work and think-pair-share allows students to learn from each other and makes the learning experience communal. Using projects and real world problems makes the course work relevant to students and allows them to show their mastery of the material and concepts in different ways.

    Incorporating Tech in Teaching

    Incorporating different technology in my classroom can aid in making my teaching more effective and my classroom more inclusive. Whether being used in a hybrid or online course or a traditional in person lecture, using the tools available to better present and assess knowledge is critical for creating an optimal teaching environment.One tool I plan to use is pre class assignments. These will be administered online prior to class as a way of giving students a preview of what we will cover during the lesson. They will consists of a short reading online or a video to watch prior to class. Students then are required to post one question they had about the reading or video.

    Understanding the University Context

    To create the right teaching environment for students it is vital that I understand the context in which I am teaching. Different educational institutions have different goals and values and it is critical that I understand what is being asked of me in order to perform my best.For example, at a Research University my role as a professor would likely include leading a research group of post docs, graduate students and undergraduate students. This leadership role would include mentorship, guiding students through their academic paths, writing and co-writing publications, teaching classes and writing grants to fund my research group.Teaching at a community college would be vastly different and including more teaching and organizing and facilitating community events.

    Assessing Student Learning

    Assessing student learning is critical for understanding my impact as a teacher. I need to know how much students understand in order to determine if my teaching is effective and what should change. The way I assess student learning may differ depending on the purpose of the course. For a lower level course meant as a general overview of concepts and not meant to prepare students for a career in STEM my assessments will be more focused on a general understanding of concepts and students application of skills to their own lives. For a higher level course meant to prepare students for upper division courses and a career in a Physics or Astronomy field the assessments will be more geared to illustrating how students use and apply their knowledge and less focused on memorization.
